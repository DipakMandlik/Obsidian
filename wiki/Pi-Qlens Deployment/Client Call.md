## Script

"Pi-Qlens is a Snowflake-native data-quality and validation platform that validates data directly where it lives."

"It brings the complete quality lifecycle together—from data profiling and configurable rules to threshold-based validation and root-cause analysis."

"Once validation is complete, Pi-Qlens turns the results into business-ready reporting, Snowflake-ready metrics, and operational notifications."

"The framework is fully deterministic and audit-ready, providing transparent results that can be traced back to the underlying rules and evaluations."

"Together, these capabilities help organizations build trusted data while simplifying the quality process within their existing Snowflake environment."

"It gives teams the flexibility to run full, incremental, or targeted scans—balancing validation coverage with efficient Snowflake compute usage."

"The validated outcomes then become actionable business intelligence through reporting, Snowflake-ready metrics, notifications, and audit evidence turning data quality into trusted, measurable business insight."

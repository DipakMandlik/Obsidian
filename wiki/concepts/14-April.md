# 14 April AI & Data Research News

This page captures key research breakthroughs, model releases, and industry developments in the Data and AI domains as of 14 April (historical context from 2024).

## 1. Major Model Releases & Open-Source Milestones
*   **Meta [[llama-3]]**: Released versions 8B and 70B, setting new benchmarks for open-source LLMs in reasoning and coding.
*   **Microsoft [[phi-3-mini]]**: A 3.8B parameter model demonstrating high performance in reasoning and logic, rivaling much larger models.
*   **Apple OpenELM**: Efficient language models (270M to 3B parameters) designed for on-device transparency.
*   **Snowflake Arctic**: An enterprise-grade Mixture-of-Experts ([[moe]]) model optimized for training efficiency.

## 2. Key Research & Technical Breakthroughs
*   **Vision Transformers ([[vit]]) with Registers**: Introduced "register tokens" to fix artifacts in ViTs, improving object detection and feature mapping.
*   **Visual Autoregressive Modeling (VAR)**: A new image generation approach predicting images in stages (coarse to fine), outperforming traditional diffusion transformers.
*   **Retrieval Augmented Fine-Tuning ([[raft]])**: A method to train LLMs to ignore distractor documents in [[rag]] pipelines, enhancing domain accuracy.
*   **Jamba (AI21 Labs)**: First production-grade hybrid architecture combining Transformer and [[mamba]] (State Space Model) layers for 256K token context windows.

## 3. Industry & Infrastructure
*   **Microsoft Investment**: Announced $2.9B in Japan and $1.7B in Indonesia for AI infrastructure.
*   **Amazon & Anthropic**: Completed a $4B investment in [[anthropic]] to deploy Claude models on AWS.
*   **Vidu (China)**: A high-definition text-to-video generator rivaling OpenAI's Sora.

## 4. Data Science & Applied AI
*   **FineWeb Dataset**: Hugging Face released a 15-trillion token dataset for training high-quality open-source LLMs.
*   **Google DeepMind GraphCast**: Significant progress in weather prediction, particularly for hurricane path accuracy using graph-based models.

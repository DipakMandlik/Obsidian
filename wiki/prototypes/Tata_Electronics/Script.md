"Welcome to the demonstration of the Pibythree Quality Hub."

"Pibythree Quality Hub is a role-based digital quality inspection platform designed to bring the complete testing and quality process onto a single platform."

"The objective is not simply to digitize a checklist. The platform creates an end-to-end workflow connecting execution, verification, management, configuration, and governance."

"There are five key roles within the platform: Quality Technician, Quality Checker, Supervisor, Template Manager, and Administrator."


1) QUALITY TECHNICIAN :
	"The first role is the Quality Technician."
> 
> "The Quality Technician is responsible for executing and recording the actual quality tests."
> 
> "The technician can view the tests assigned to them and their execution schedule."
> 
> "Before starting the test, the technician verifies the relevant location and station."
> 
> "The technician then performs the quality checks according to the digital worksheet, captures the required evidence such as photographs or videos, and enters the corresponding results."
> 
> "If a failure occurs, the technician records the failure category, severity, and description."
> 
> "The platform automatically saves progress and only allows the test to be submitted once all required checks have been completed."
> 
> "So, the primary responsibility of the Quality Technician is simple: **accurate execution and complete data capture.**"



2) QUALITY CHECKER
"The second role is the Quality Checker."

"The Quality Checker provides the independent quality verification layer."

"Once a technician completes a test, the Quality Checker can see the tests that are pending verification."

"The checker reviews the complete result, including the recorded values, evidence, and any failure details."

"Based on the review, the Quality Checker can approve the test, reject it, or request a retest."

"The checker can also provide comments and feedback and ensure that the inspection meets the required quality standards."

"This creates an important separation between **execution and verification**."

"The technician performs the test; the Quality Checker validates the result."


3) SUPERVISOR 
	"The third role is the Supervisor."

"The Supervisor is responsible for planning, monitoring, and managing the operational workflow."

"The Supervisor can create assignments by defining the unit, technician, template, station, due date, and priority."

"The Supervisor can monitor testing progress and team performance and reassign tests when required."

"The Supervisor also has visibility into pending, completed, failed, and retest statuses."

"An important capability here is that the Supervisor can understand which Quality Checker is associated with a particular Quality Technician and can manage that assignment."

"This creates a clear operational hierarchy between execution and verification."

"The Supervisor therefore acts as the control layer that ensures work is assigned correctly and completed on time."



4) TEMPLATE MANAGER
	"The fourth role is the Template Manager."

"The Template Manager is responsible for designing and maintaining the digital quality templates used during testing."

"Templates can be imported from Excel or created directly within the platform."

"The Template Manager can configure categories, checks, instructions, rules, test types, evidence requirements, measurements, and failure categories."

"Before publishing a template, the Template Manager can preview it from the perspective of the tester."

"Templates can then be validated, versioned, published, and maintained through their revisions."

"This ensures that technicians always work with standardized and controlled testing instructions."



5) ADMINISTRATOR
"The fifth role is the Administrator."

"The Administrator is responsible for the overall configuration and governance of the platform."

"This includes managing users, roles, and permissions."

"The Administrator can configure plants, locations, stations, devices, and units."

"The role also manages failure categories, reference data, application settings, and master data."

"In addition, the Administrator has visibility into system audit trails and activity logs."

"The focus of this role is therefore system integrity, security, governance, and controlled configuration."


"In summary, five roles work together on one platform to ensure quality, traceability, and operational excellence."

"Now, let's see this workflow in action."

# RECORDING DIRECTION — IMPORTANT

## Do not narrate every click

The recording should explain **business purpose**, not UI mechanics.

Instead of saying:

> "Now I click this button."

Say:

> "The Supervisor can create an assignment by defining the technician, template, station, due date, and priority."

---

## Use the same terminology throughout

Always use:

- **Quality Technician**
- **Quality Checker**
- **Supervisor**
- **Template Manager**
- **Administrator**

Do not switch between "Tester" and "Technician" during the recording.

---

# THE CORE STORY TO REMEMBER

If you forget any part of the narration while recording, come back to this:

> **Administrator configures the platform.**
> 
> **Template Manager defines what needs to be tested.**
> 
> **Supervisor assigns and manages the work.**
> 
> **Quality Technician executes the test.**
> 
> **Quality Checker verifies the result.**
> 
> **Supervisor monitors the complete operation.**
> 
> **The platform maintains traceability throughout the entire lifecycle.**

This should be the **central narrative of the entire demo**, so the audience understands the platform as one connected enterprise workflow rather than five independent role dashboards.

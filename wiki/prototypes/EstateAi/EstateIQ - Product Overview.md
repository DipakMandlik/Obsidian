# EstateIQ

## AI-Powered Real Estate Intelligence & Decision Platform

### Pibythree Technologies — Real Estate & Construction AI Capability Showcase

> [!quote] One-Line Description
> **EstateIQ is an AI-powered real-estate intelligence and decision platform that connects property, tenant, contract, project and market information to help business teams identify risks, discover opportunities and take informed actions.**

---

## 1. Project Overview

**EstateIQ** is Pibythree Technologies' AI-powered real-estate intelligence and decision-support prototype, designed to demonstrate how AI can help commercial real-estate and construction organizations move from fragmented operational data to actionable business decisions.

> [!important] Core Business Principle
> **Data → Intelligence → Insight → Recommendation → Action**

Instead of a generic real-estate dashboard or a standalone document chatbot, EstateIQ brings together multiple real-estate business domains and presents intelligence according to the responsibilities of different business **personas**.

### What the platform helps users do

- Understand property and tenant information
- Extract intelligence from contracts and documents
- Identify contractual risks and obligations
- Monitor project and construction progress
- Validate project milestones
- Support payment decisions
- Evaluate investment and development opportunities
- Identify potential corporate leasing prospects
- Prioritize business actions
- Connect actions across different business personas

> [!warning] Positioning
> EstateIQ is currently an **advanced enterprise prototype / capability showcase**, using **realistic fictional data**. It is **not** a production deployment and is **not** connected to a real customer's ERP, CRM, or document systems.

## 2. Core Business Vision

Real-estate organizations operate across a large, fragmented information landscape spanning:

- Property management systems

- ERP systems
- Lease agreements & amendments

- Project schedules
- Contractor submissions
- Site reports & construction photographs
- Financial information

- Corporate expansion signals

Different teams consume the **same underlying information** differently:

| Persona | Core Question |
|---|---|

**EstateIQ provides a common intelligence layer for these questions.**

## 11. Contract Intelligence Use Case

A tenant relationship can contain a chain:

```text
Original Agreement → Amendment 01 → Amendment 02 → Current Contract Position
```

Extracted information: Tenant, property, lease term, lock-in, rent, escalation, security deposit, notice period, renewal, termination conditions.

### Amendment Intelligence — example comparison

| Clause | Previous | Current |
|---|---|---|
| Notice Period | 90 days | 120 days |
| Escalation | 10% | 12% |
| Termination | Standard | Modified |

The platform highlights: **What changed → Why it matters → Potential business impact → Risk level → Relevant clause/evidence.**

> [!important] AI does not merely summarize a contract; it understands **changes** and their **business implications**.

## 13. Contract Obligation Management

Converts contract information into operational events.

> [!example]
> Contract says: *"12% rent escalation effective 1 October 2026."*
>
> EstateIQ creates a **Contract Obligation**:
>
> | Field | Value |
> |---|---|
> | Type | Rent Escalation |
> | Owner | Leasing / Finance |
> | Due | 1 Oct 2026 |
> | Priority | High |

```text
Contract → Obligation → Event → Responsible Team → Action
```

## 29. AI Response Structure

> [!info] Intended Response Pattern
> **Insight** (what happened?) → **Evidence** (what data supports it?) → **Recommendation** (what should happen?) → **Action** (what can the user do?)

> [!example]
> *"Apex Digital's renewal is a high-priority opportunity because the lease expires within the defined renewal window and current rent is below the market benchmark."*
>
> **Evidence:** Lease expiry · Market rent · Current rent · Renewal probability
> **Recommendation:** Initiate renewal discussions.

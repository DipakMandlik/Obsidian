# National Planning Council (NPC), State of Qatar

## Who is NPC?

The **National Planning Council (NPC), State of Qatar** was established under **Amiri Decision No. 13 of 2024**. It is responsible for aligning national strategy, data, planning, performance, and foresight in support of Qatar's development agenda.

## NPC mandate

1. **National vision and strategy**
   - Develop Qatar's national visions, strategies, and development plans.
   - Support progress toward **Qatar National Vision 2030**.

2. **Implementation oversight**
   - Supervise implementation of national strategies and plans.
   - Track delivery, progress, and performance across entities and sectors.

3. **Government data and official statistics**
   - Strengthen the data and evidence base for national planning and decision-making.
   - Monitor Qatar's performance in international indices.

4. **Policy, foresight, and risk management**
   - Monitor future developments across sectors.
   - Support proactive planning, risk identification, and informed policy decisions.

## Why NPC is relevant to π by3

NPC sits at the intersection of:

- National strategy
- Government data
- Official statistics
- Policy
- Performance monitoring
- Foresight
- Decision intelligence

For NPC, a platform must do more than record activities. It should create governed workflows, reliable evidence, clear accountability, and traceable information for timely decisions.

# syngo Carbon — Qatar Healthcare Context

## What is syngo Carbon?

**syngo Carbon** is Siemens Healthineers' enterprise imaging and clinical multimedia data-management platform. In a Qatar healthcare setting, it can help hospitals centrally manage, access, visualize, and exchange medical images and related clinical multimedia information across departments.

It is broader than a traditional **Picture Archiving and Communication System (PACS)**. PACS primarily focuses on radiology image storage and retrieval, while syngo Carbon is designed as an enterprise imaging ecosystem that can bring together imaging and related information from multiple clinical specialties through integrated workflows.

## Typical logical flow

1. **Imaging modalities and clinical departments**
   - CT
   - MRI
   - X-ray
   - Ultrasound
   - Cardiology
   - Other diagnostic and clinical imaging services

2. **syngo Carbon — enterprise imaging layer**
   - Image and clinical multimedia management
   - Storage and secure access
   - Clinical viewing and visualization
   - Workflow orchestration and reporting
   - Clinical collaboration

3. **Hospital clinical systems**
   - HIS
   - RIS
   - EHR / EMR
   - Other healthcare applications

4. **Clinicians and specialists**
   - Radiologists
   - Cardiologists
   - Physicians
   - Other authorized clinical users

## π by3 opportunity perspective

syngo Carbon should generally **not** be viewed as a core platform that π by3 needs to replace. Where a Qatar healthcare organization already uses Siemens Healthineers and syngo Carbon, π by3's more relevant opportunity is to **complement the existing imaging ecosystem**.

### Potential π by3 value around the platform

| Area | Potential π by3 contribution |
|---|---|
| **Interoperability and integration** | Connect available syngo Carbon interfaces with hospital, clinical, operational, and external systems. |
| **Trusted healthcare data** | Consolidate appropriately governed data from syngo Carbon and other clinical systems into a trusted data foundation. |
| **Data engineering and cloud** | Build secure pipelines, data models, and cloud or hybrid data capabilities around the existing imaging environment. |
| **Analytics and clinical intelligence** | Enable operational analytics, performance monitoring, and appropriately governed clinical intelligence use cases. |
| **Automation** | Automate approved data flows, workflow handoffs, alerts, and reporting processes. |
| **AI enablement** | Support controlled AI use cases using approved, governed data and defined clinical oversight. |

## Integration and governance considerations

Any integration must be assessed against the specific syngo Carbon deployment and the interfaces or APIs available to the healthcare organization. It should also comply with applicable Qatar healthcare requirements for:

- Healthcare data privacy and confidentiality
- Cybersecurity and access control
- Data residency and hosting requirements
- Clinical governance and authorization
- Regulatory and organizational policies

## Key takeaway

> **syngo Carbon is Siemens Healthineers' enterprise imaging platform.**

> **For a Qatar healthcare client already using syngo Carbon, π by3's opportunity is likely to sit around the platform: integration, trusted healthcare data, analytics, automation, cloud, and responsibly governed AI—not replacement of the core imaging system.**

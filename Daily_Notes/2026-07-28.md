				4. **Personal Lending**
			    - Offers unsecured personal loans to consumers for purposes such as debt consolidation, home improvements, or major expenses.

3) DecisionOps AI :
	1) Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform powered by Databricks Agent Bricks
	2) What is DecisionOps AI? :
		1) DecisionOps AI is an enterprise platform that continuously monitors structured and unstructured enterprise information, understands business context, reasons across multiple data sources, identifies risks and opportunities, and produces explainable recommendations for human decision-makers.
		2) Instead of waiting for a user to ask a question, the platform continuously analyzes newly arriving information and determines whether it requires attention.
		3) It behaves more like an experienced analyst than a search engine
	3) Scenarios :
		1) BSE Scenario :
			1) Current Business: 
				The Bombay Stock Exchange receives and manages a continuous stream of information from listed companies and market participants.
			2) Examples include:
				   1. Annual Reports
				2. Quarterly Results
				3. Board Meeting Outcomes
				4. Corporate Announcements
				5. Shareholding Patterns
				6. Insider Trading Disclosures
				7. Regulatory Filings
				8. Investor Presentations
				9. Press Releases
				10. SEBI Communications
				11. Market News
				
				Every document has the potential to influence markets or require regulatory attention.
			 1) Current  Challenges:
				 1) Analysts and compliance teams manually review these documents to determine:
					1. Is there a material event?
					2. Has company performance changed significantly?
					3. Is there a governance concern?
					4. Does this disclosure conflict with previous filings?
					5. Should investors or regulators pay attention ?
				This process is time-consuming and becomes increasingly difficult as document volumes grow.
			2) DecisionOps AI for BSE :
				When a new filing arrives, the platform automatically:
				- Classifies the document.
				- Extracts key entities and financial metrics.
				- Compares the filing with historical disclosures.
				- Identifies unusual changes.
				- Correlates the filing with recent news and market events.
				- Evaluates whether the event is materially significant.
				- Produces an explainable analyst brief.
				- Routes high-priority cases to the appropriate review team.
				The objective is not to replace analysts, but to help them focus on the filings that matter most.
				1) Example
					A listed company uploads its quarterly results.
					The platform identifies:
					- Revenue declined 18%.
					- Debt increased substantially.
					- Auditor remarks have changed.
					- Promoter shareholding has decreased.
					- Recent news reports mention a manufacturing disruption.
					Instead of five separate reviews, the platform presents one evidence-backed analysis with supporting references and recommended next steps.
			3) Sun Pharma Scenario :
				1) Current Business
					Sun Pharma operates in a highly regulated manufacturing environment where every product batch and manufacturing process is documented.
					Examples include:
					- SOPs
					- Batch Manufacturing Records
					- Laboratory Results
					- CAPA Reports
					- Deviation Reports
					- Validation Documents
					- Equipment Logs
					- Change Controls
					- Internal Audits
					- FDA Inspection Observations
					Maintaining quality and regulatory compliance depends on accurate interpretation of these documents.
				2) Current Challenge
					Quality and compliance teams manually investigate:
					- Why did a deviation occur?
					- Which SOP applies?
					- Has a similar issue occurred before?
					- Which batches may be affected?
					- What corrective action should be taken?
					- Could this lead to regulatory findings?
					The information is spread across many systems and document types, making investigations slow and resource-intensive.
				3) DecisionOps AI for Sun Pharma
					When a new quality event is reported, the platform:
						- Identifies the affected process.
						- Retrieves the relevant SOPs.
						- Finds similar historical deviations.
						- Reviews associated CAPAs.
						- Correlates equipment, batch, and quality records.
						- Assesses potential regulatory impact.
						- Suggests evidence-backed corrective actions.
						- Produces an investigation summary for quality reviewers.
					The platform supports quality professionals by accelerating investigations while maintaining traceability and human oversight.
				 Example : A manufacturing deviation is reported.
					The platform determines:
									- The deviation matches two previous incidents.
									- The same equipment was involved.
									- A related CAPA was closed six months earlier.
									- The applicable SOP was not fully followed.
									- Three additional batches may require review.
					The investigator receives a consolidated analysis rather than assembling this information manually from multiple systems.
